Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

shorts-agent ("the app") is a private tool used by its owner to publish short videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with that information.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests permission to:

  • upload videos to the signed-in YouTube channel;
  • read basic information and statistics (views, likes, comments) for videos on that channel;
  • delete videos that the app itself uploaded to that channel.

The app also reads publicly available YouTube search results to find trending videos.

How the information is used and stored

This information is used only to upload videos, track how they perform, and remove them when needed. Sign-in tokens and video statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app does not sell, share or transfer any of this information to anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page, security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Deleting the app's local data folder removes all stored tokens and statistics.
